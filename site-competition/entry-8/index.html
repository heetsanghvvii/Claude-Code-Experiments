The problem

Hundreds of applications. No callbacks.

The portal

A queue.

Your application joins thousands of others and waits for a filter to read it. Nobody is choosing you. Nobody even knows you are there.

The door

People hire people.

One person inside the company who knows your name can pass on your CV, put in a word, or simply tell you who to talk to. That is how most good roles actually move.

How it works

Four steps. You hold the key at every one.

You tell us where you want to be. We do the finding, the research and the writing. Nothing reaches anyone until you say yes.

  1. STEP 01

    You pick the targets

    The companies you want, the roles you want there. A short form, nothing more.

  2. STEP 02

    We find the right people

    Hiring managers, future teammates, alumni from your school, and senior people who can introduce you.

  3. STEP 03

    We write to each one

    A short, personal first message built on something you genuinely share. You approve it, then it goes from your account.

  4. STEP 04

    We guide every reply

    When someone answers, we draft your next message and help the conversation along until it becomes a referral or an interview.

Questions

Is this spam?

No. Spam is the same message sent to thousands. We contact around 60 researched people at a time, and every message is written for that one person.

We stay well under LinkedIn's limits, and you send from your own account, at a human pace. There are no bots logging in as you.

Do you write as me?

Yes, in your voice. And you approve every message before it goes. Nothing is ever sent without your yes.
