How it works

Four steps from a list of companies to a conversation.

  1. 01 You

    Pick your targets

    Tell us the companies and roles you want. Ten companies is a good start.

  2. 02 We

    Find the right people

    Hiring managers, team members, alumni and senior people who can introduce you, at each company.

  3. 03 We

    Write a first message

    One for each person, built on something you genuinely share. Short, specific, never a template.

  4. 04 Together

    Guide every reply

    We draft your responses and steer each conversation until it becomes a referral or an interview.

You approve every message. Nothing goes out without you, and it is sent from your own LinkedIn.
Why it works
60 people.Hand-picked. Not 6,000 messages.
  • i.

    A short list, chosen by hand

    We would rather reach the right sixty than spray thousands. Fewer people, far more care.

  • ii.

    Every message is written for one person

    Their path, their team, the thing you share. If it could be sent to anyone, we do not send it.

  • iii.

    Conversation first, the ask comes later

    Nobody likes a stranger asking for a referral. People do like talking about their own work.

  • iv.

    Built on how modern sales teams create meetings

    The same research-led outbound that books meetings with busy executives, applied to your career.
