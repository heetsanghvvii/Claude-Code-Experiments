## What it means for nope (hypotheses)
1. The loudest objection is exactly the one we will face at Rs 1,999: "it is just an NFC chip". We must answer it in the first seconds and on the landing page: the chip is Rs 20, the product is the finished puck, a safe app (backup code, emergency unlock, essentials always allowed, setup help) and support. Say it honestly, do not pretend the chip is special.
2. The free alternatives are real (Foqos, TapBlok, nfcGuard, OneSec, Samsung modes) and named by strangers. Locking the app to our pucks is a weak moat; the package and brand must carry the value (CMO decision 1 in the CEO files should be revisited with this in mind).
3. Bypass is the number two worry and the funniest. We say honestly that we reduce impulsive opens and do not make cheating impossible. Strict mode for APK installs answers the "I'll uninstall it" crowd, but Google Play forbids using accessibility to stop uninstalling (confirmed in the Instinct research), so strict mode stays off in Play builds.
4. Many alternatives mentioned are iPhone ones (Shortcuts, Foqos). The audience on this reel skews iPhone, which supports flagging iPhone timing to the CEO and CSO.
5. A number of buyers are real and enthusiastic, so a physical block has pull among people who want a finished product.
6. The price people name for Brick in India is about Rs 6,000 ("isn't it 6k"). The creator says she paid Rs 11,000, likely more than one unit or extras; unverified.
