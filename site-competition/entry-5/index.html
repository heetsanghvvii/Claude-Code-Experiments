How it works

Four steps from target list to interview.

  1. Door 01

    You pick your targets

    Tell us the companies and roles you want. A shortlist is better than a wishlist.

  2. Door 02

    We find the right people

    Hiring managers, future teammates, alumni from your school, and senior people who can introduce you.

  3. Door 03

    We write each first message

    One short, personal note per person, built on something you genuinely share.

  4. Door 04

    We draft your responses and steer each conversation until it becomes a referral or an interview.

You approve every message. Nothing is sent without your yes, and everything goes from your own LinkedIn.
