# Knock landing page brief

Every contestant builds from this brief alone. It is complete: do not ask questions, make the calls yourself.

## The product
**Knock** is a career outbound service for job seekers in India, starting with MBA graduates and people moving into product management. Instead of applying through job portals, Knock finds the right people at a candidate's target companies (hiring managers, team members, alumni, senior people who can introduce), researches each one, finds a genuine shared reason to talk, writes a short personal first message, and guides every reply until it becomes a referral or an interview. The candidate approves every message; it is sent from their own LinkedIn.

Positioning: a premium, human, tech-enabled **service**, not an "AI tool". AI is how it is delivered, never the headline. Think private career concierge, not growth-hacking software.

North-star promise: **interviews at your target companies, through the people who work there.**

## Audience
MBA graduates and final-year students (IIMs, ISB, other top schools), and professionals switching into product roles. Ambitious, skeptical of spam, frustrated after sending hundreds of applications with no reply. Mostly on mobile.

## Brand
- Name: Knock. Tagline: "Knock on the right door."
- Voice: short, warm, confident, plain English. No hype, no exclamation marks, no em dashes.
- Feel: calm, premium, human. No robot imagery, no "AI-powered" badges, no purple AI gradients.
- Visual direction is yours to decide within that feel.

## Page goal
One primary action: **Get my target list** (opens or scrolls to a short intake form). Use this exact label for the primary CTA everywhere.

## Content to include (write final copy; you may tighten wording)
1. **Hero**: headline in the spirit of "Stop applying. Start conversations." Subline: we find the hiring managers, team members and alumni at your target companies, research each one, and start real conversations that turn into referrals and interviews. CTA: Get my target list.
2. **The problem**: hundreds of applications, no callbacks. Job portals are a queue; people hire people.
3. **How it works** (a real sequence, 4 steps): you pick your target companies and roles; we find the right people at each; we write a personal first message for each person, based on something you genuinely share; we guide every reply until it becomes a referral or interview. You approve every message.
4. **Why it works**: 60 hand-picked people, not 6,000 messages. Every message is written for one person. Conversations first, the ask comes later. Built on how modern sales teams create meetings.
5. **An example first message** (show it as a realistic message, not a fake app screenshot): "Hey Rahul, saw you moved from Flipkart into product at Zepto. I'm making a similar jump from brand management. Curious, what was the biggest adjustment for you?"
6. **Packages** (founding member pricing, INR, no reply guarantees yet):
   - Sprint, 7 days, 60 people contacted, Rs 2,499
   - Standard, 14 days, 120 people contacted, Rs 3,999 (most popular)
   - Full search, 21 days, 180 people contacted, Rs 5,499
   - All include: target list, research on every person, personal first messages, reply drafts for every conversation, weekly results report.
   - Done-for-you option: we run the outreach end to end, from Rs 14,999.
7. **FAQ**: Is this spam? (No: 60 researched people, every message personal.) Will my LinkedIn get banned? (We stay well under LinkedIn's limits; you send from your own account.) Do you write as me? (Yes, and you approve every message before it goes.) Who is this for? (MBA grads and people moving into product roles, to start.) What if nobody replies? (Founding members get a free extra week if fewer than 5 people reply.)
8. **Intake form** (static is fine, no backend): full name, email, LinkedIn URL, target role, target companies (comma separated), city. Submit button: Get my target list. Show a clear success state on submit.
9. **Footer**: Knock, the tagline, contact email hello@knock.careers.

No testimonials, logos, user counts or statistics: Knock has no customers yet, so do not invent any.

## Technical rules (all contestants)
- Deliver ONE self-contained file: `index.html` in your entry folder. Inline all CSS and JS.
- External requests: Google Fonts only (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). No other CDNs, no external images (they are blocked here and break the page). Use CSS, inline SVG or clearly labeled placeholders for visuals.
- If your skill builds with a toolchain (React, Tailwind, bundler), that is fine as long as the final output is the single inlined `index.html`.
- Responsive down to 390px wide, no horizontal scroll, visible keyboard focus, respects prefers-reduced-motion, WCAG AA contrast.
- Also write `NOTES.md` in your folder: design direction in 3 lines, key decisions, anything you could not do.
