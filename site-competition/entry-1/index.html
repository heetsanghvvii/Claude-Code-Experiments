How it works

Four steps, done with you. You stay in control of every word sent in your name.

  1. You pick the companies and roles

    Tell us where you want to work and what you want to do there. That is the brief we work from.

  2. We find the right people at each

    Hiring managers, future teammates, alumni and senior people who can introduce you. Then we research every one of them.

  3. We write a personal first message

    One per person, built on something you genuinely share: a school, a past company, the same career move.

  4. We guide every reply

    We draft your responses and steer each conversation until it becomes a referral or an interview.

You approve every message. It goes from your own LinkedIn.
