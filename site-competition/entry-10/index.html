The problem

Is this spam?

No. We contact around 60 researched people per week of your search, and every message is written for that one person. Spam is the opposite: thousands of copies of the same note.

Will my LinkedIn get banned?

We stay well under LinkedIn's limits, and you send from your own account at a human pace. No bots, no automation running on your profile.

Do you write as me?

Yes, in your voice. You approve every message before it goes, and you can edit or skip anything.

Who is this for?

To start, MBA graduates and final-year students, and professionals moving into product roles.

What if nobody replies?
