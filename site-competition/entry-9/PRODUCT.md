# Knock

Career outbound service for job seekers in India (MBA graduates, people moving into product). Knock finds the right people at a candidate's target companies, researches each, writes a personal first message, and guides every reply to a referral or interview. Candidate approves every message; it is sent from their own LinkedIn.

- Positioning: premium, human service. AI is delivery, never headline.
- Promise: interviews at your target companies, through the people who work there.
- Audience: ambitious, spam-skeptical, mostly on mobile.
- Voice: short, warm, confident, plain. No hype, no exclamation marks, no em dashes.
- Primary action: "Get my target list".
- No customers yet: no testimonials, logos, counts or statistics.
