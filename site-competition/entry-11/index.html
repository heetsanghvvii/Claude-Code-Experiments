Why it works

Fewer messages. Better doors.

60 6,000

Hand-picked people, not mass messages.

Each person is chosen for a reason: they can hire you, work with you, or vouch for you. Sixty careful conversations beat thousands of ignored ones.

Every message is written for one person.

No templates with a name swapped in. If it could be sent to anyone, we do not send it.

Conversations first. The ask comes later.

People help people they have spoken to. We open with curiosity, and ask for a referral only once there is a real exchange.

Built on how modern sales teams create meetings.

The best sales teams do not cold call everyone. They research a few, find a reason to talk, and follow up with care. We do the same for your career.

Questions

Is this spam?

No. We contact around 60 researched people per week, and every message is written for that one person. Spam is the opposite: thousands of identical messages.

We stay well under LinkedIn's limits, and you send every message from your own account at a normal, human pace.

Do you write as me?

Yes, in your voice. And you approve every message before it goes. Nothing is sent without you.

Who is this for?

What if nobody replies?
