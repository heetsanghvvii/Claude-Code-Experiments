# Client Intake Questionnaire

Tight answers keep each client under 50 prospects and inside free tool limits.

## Files
1. Resume / CV (PDF)
2. Your LinkedIn profile as PDF (LinkedIn profile page, "More", "Save to PDF")

## Targeting
3. Exact target role titles (max 2). Example: "Product Manager", "Associate Product Manager"
4. Target companies (5 to 8). Rank them.
5. City or cities you would work in (max 2). Remote OK? (yes / no)
6. Industries you care about (max 3)
7. Years of experience
8. Anything off-limits: companies, current employer, people you already know there

## Story
9. In one line, why this role, why now?
10. The 2 projects you are proudest of, with one number each
11. Any career transition you are making (e.g. consulting to product)
12. Communities you belong to: schools, clubs, ex-employers, hometown, sports, causes

## Preferences
13. Tone: formal, neutral, or casual
14. Things you never want said in a message
