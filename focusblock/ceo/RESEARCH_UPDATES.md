# Research updates for the CEO (9 Nov 2026, from the Instinct report and the reel comments)

Sources: docs/research/NOPE-India-Research-9-Nov-2026.pdf (9 items, read only, many items rated high confidence), marketing/reference/REEL_AN_HAD_FUN.md and COMMENTS_ANALYSIS.md. Labels: VERIFIED by Instinct (with source URL in the PDF), UNVERIFIED, NEW.

## 1. Platform and legal (VERIFIED unless stated)
- Google Play personal developer accounts need a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for the preceding 14 days before production access. Fee US$25 one time (INR unverified).
- Accessibility service: a blocker can be eligible but is not an accessibility tool, so it needs a prominent in app disclosure with affirmative consent, a Play Console declaration with a video, deterministic rule based behaviour only, and the policy FORBIDS using it to prevent users disabling or uninstalling an app. So strict mode must never ship in a Play build (CEO D21 stands).
- Android 13 and up sideload flow confirmed: Settings > Apps > the app > three dots > Allow restricted settings, then enable the service. Put this in SETUP_GUIDE and the setup doctor (already drafted).
- Trademark: the search needs a login (OTP) so Instinct could not run it. The founder must search himself. Official fee: Rs 4,500 per class for e-filing as an individual or startup (Rs 5,000 physical); three classes (9, 35, 42) = Rs 13,500. Not budgeted yet.
- GST: working candidate heading 8523 at 18 percent (not a ruling); there is NO rule that every online goods seller registers from the first sale; a conditional exemption exists below the threshold (goods Rs 40 lakh for most states including Maharashtra, Rs 20 lakh in ten listed states) but it is tied to conditions (marketplace route, no inter-State supply) that may not fit a direct website. Phase 1 revenue (about Rs 1 lakh) is far below the threshold. NEW: CFO should treat "not registered" as a live case, not only "registered"; a CA call decides.
- Legal Metrology box declarations (VERIFIED baseline): manufacturer name and address, country of origin if imported, generic name, net quantity as a count, month AND year of manufacture (mandatory since 1 Jan 2024), MRP inclusive of all taxes, consumer complaint contact (name, address, phone, email), dimensions if relevant, unit price where required. Add to PACKAGING.md and the box artwork.

## 2. Market
- Brick: Instinct saw US$59 for one (no INR shown); the founder's own screenshot on the Indian site showed Rs 5,700. Both may be right (currency localisation). Shipping and duty to India unverified. The creator says she paid Rs 11,000. Do not quote Brick prices in ads.
- Unpluq (India App Store): Rs 499 per month or Rs 4,999 per year subscription. Others named: Spark, bloc (India, fridge style), BLOCC, Monk (EUR 54.95), free apps TapBlok, nfcGuard and Foqos. No Amazon.in data (pages did not load). Flipkart page 1 shows only blank NFC stickers at Rs 239 to Rs 394 for packs. Treat "no Indian physical blocker on Flipkart page 1" as medium confidence only.
- NFC share of Android phones in India: no published figure exists (do not quote a percentage). Catalogue shows NFC phones start around Rs 9,799. Keep the NFC check on the website and in the app.
- Meta ads India: CPM about Rs 120 to Rs 210 (low to medium confidence), one ecommerce source gives CPC about Rs 11.25. At Rs 11.25 per click the break even landing page conversion is about 1.5 percent (CAC 726 divided into 11.25), versus 3.4 percent at the Rs 25 per view the CEO used. Still unproven; the Rs 1,000 test will show the real figure. Rs 1,000 buys roughly 75 to 90 clicks at that CPC.
