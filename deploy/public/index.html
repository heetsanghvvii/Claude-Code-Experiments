How it works

Four steps, from a list of companies to a conversation with someone inside.

  1. 1

    You pick your targets

    Tell us the companies and roles you want. Ten companies is plenty.

  2. 2

    We find the right people

    Hiring managers, future teammates, alumni and senior people who can introduce you. Researched one by one.

  3. 3

    We write to each one

    A short, personal first message, built on something you genuinely share.

  4. 4

    We guide every reply

    We draft your answers and steer each conversation until it becomes a referral or an interview.

You approve every message. It goes from your own LinkedIn, in your own name.

Why it works

Fewer, better doors. The same way modern sales teams get meetings with people who have never heard of them.

  • 60 hand-picked people, not 6,000 messages

    A short list of people who can actually move your application, each chosen for a reason.

  • Every message is written for one person

    No templates with a name swapped in. If it could be sent to anyone, we do not send it.

  • Conversations first. The ask comes later.

    Nobody refers a stranger. We open with curiosity, earn the reply, and ask for the referral once there is something real between you.

  • Built on how sales teams create meetings

    Research, relevance, follow-up. The way good sales teams reach busy people, applied to the one product you know best: you.

What a first message sounds like

Short. Specific. About them. A reason to reply, and nothing to say no to.

  1. 1Proves we looked. It names something real about their path, so it cannot be mistaken for a mass message.
  2. 2Gives a reason to talk. A shared move makes you a peer, not a stranger asking for a favour.
  3. 3Asks something easy. One question they can answer in a line. No referral ask yet.
