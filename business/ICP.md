# Knock: target clients (ICP)

Knock is for anyone whose next job is at a company where knowing someone inside matters. That covers most white-collar hiring in India, not just MBA graduates.

**Positioning line:** For professionals who know where they want to work, and want a real conversation with the people there instead of another application.

## Who it works for (all 5 must be true)
1. **Clear targets:** they can name 10 to 30 companies (or we build the list with them).
2. **Referral-driven roles:** jobs where a referral or a hiring manager conversation moves the needle: tech, startups, consulting, product, growth, strategy, data, design, marketing, sales, finance, engineering.
3. **A real story:** a LinkedIn profile with something genuine to connect on (school, employer, city, a transition, work they shipped).
4. **Willing to approve fast:** reviews messages within 24 hours and sends (Tier 1) or lets us send (Tier 2).
5. **Can pay Rs 2,499 to Rs 19,999 upfront.**

## Segments, ranked by launch priority
| # | Segment | Why they buy | Best tier | Main channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Career switchers, 2 to 10 yrs** (into product, growth, strategy, data, tech) | Portals filter them out; a human conversation is the only way in | Tier 1 Standard | LinkedIn content, communities |
| 2 | **Laid-off or between jobs, 3 to 15 yrs** | Urgent, time-boxed, will pay for speed | Tier 1 Sprint, Tier 2 | LinkedIn #opentowork, layoff lists, alumni groups |
| 3 | **MBA graduates and final-year students** | Off-campus or post-placement search; strong alumni hooks | Tier 1 Standard | B-school clubs, placement committees |
| 4 | **Senior professionals, 12+ yrs** (director, VP, CXO) | Roles are rarely advertised; no time to do outreach themselves | Tier 2 Done-for-you | Founder network, referrals |
| 5 | **Indians abroad returning to India** | No local network left; strong reason to talk | Tier 2 | NRI and diaspora communities |
| 6 | **Fresh graduates from known colleges** targeting startups | Alumni at startups reply; cheap to serve | Tier 1 Sprint | College groups, placement cells |

**Launch focus (first 10 clients):** segments 1 and 2. They feel the pain most, decide alone and pay fast. Segment 3 runs in parallel through the free 5-student club pilot.

## Not for (say no)
- High-volume or mass-hiring roles (BPO, retail, gig), where hiring runs on portals and walk-ins.
- Government and PSU jobs (exams, not referrals).
- People who want a guaranteed job or want to blast hundreds of messages.
- People with no target and no willingness to pick one.
- Anyone who will not approve their own messages.

## Signals of a great client
- Already has 2 to 3 dream companies named in the intake form.
- Has a specific transition story (brand to product, engineer to PM, consulting to startup).
- Recently laid off or on notice period (urgency).
- Active on LinkedIn (accepts and replies fast, which helps the conversation).
