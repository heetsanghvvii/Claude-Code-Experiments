Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Is this spam?

No. Spam is the same message to thousands of strangers. We contact around 60 researched people per week, and every message is written for that one person.

Will my LinkedIn get banned?

We stay well under LinkedIn's limits, and messages go from your own account at a human pace. Nothing automated runs on your profile.

Do you write as me?

Yes, in your voice and from your story. You approve every message before it goes, and you can edit anything.

Who is this for?

To start, MBA graduates and final-year students, and professionals moving into product roles. If that is you, we would like to help.

What if nobody replies?

Founding members get a free extra week if fewer than 5 people reply. We would rather keep working than leave you waiting.
