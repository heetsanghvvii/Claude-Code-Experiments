# Fundraising readiness

Author: CSO. Date: 9 Nov 2026. Labels: FACT (repo), ASSUMPTION, UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Honest view

- Three outside AI reviews all said pass; 0 of 18 investor votes were yes [FACT, brief]. That is the correct answer for a pre-launch product with zero customers, zero revenue, a solo part-time founder, and an unrun app [FACT, plan].
- Do not raise institutional money now. Each pass costs credibility, and the plan does not yet answer the first diligence questions (does it work, does anyone pay, does anyone keep using it).
- Phase 1 needs little cash: BOM fixed cost about Rs 20,000 for 50 units and demand test spend of a few thousand to about Rs 20,000 [FACT, BOM and DEMAND_TEST]. The MD (founder and investor) can fund this. Outside money is not needed to learn.
- Raising is useful only if the numbers say we should scale. Hardware that scales needs working capital (stock and ads) before cash comes in, so the real need appears around 500 units, not 50 [my reasoning].
- Venture-scale outcome is not the only valid result. If the evidence says "good small brand", say so and plan for a profitable small business, friends and family, or strategic angels, not a priced venture round.

## 3. How to raise (when the time comes)

1. First: friends and family or small angels with operating experience in Indian consumer hardware. Cheapest, fastest, least dilution pressure.
2. Second: revenue-based financing or inventory financing once monthly sales are steady. Fits hardware with clear unit margin. Availability and terms UNVERIFIED.
3. Third: seed funds only if we have a wedge channel and retention evidence.
4. Before any outside money: incorporate (private limited or LLP), assign IP to the company, clear the employer contract, file the trademark, close GST questions [COMPLIANCE says move to a company before raising [FACT]].
5. Do not use a convertible note or valuation cap before we know the price of risk. Take advice from a lawyer on structure. UNVERIFIED.

## 4. What proof an investor needs (and when we can show it)

| Question an investor asks | Proof | Available when |
|---|---|---|
| Does it work? | A video of the tap on 5 or more real phones, test log | After R1 test (about 16 Nov) |
| Does anyone pay? | Stranger payments from ads, with order codes matching Meta clicks | After demand ladder step 2 |
| Is it profitable per unit? | One model: price, GST, BOM, refunds, shipping, gateway, founder time, measured CAC | After step 3 |
| Do people keep using it? | Day 7, 14, 28 check-ins for at least 30 buyers | About 8 weeks after first delivery |
| Do they refund? | Refund rate and reasons from the first 50 | After the first 30 day window |
| Is there a moat? | A signed institution pilot, a growing referral share, or measurable retention lift | Around rung 2 |
| Is the founder committed? | Dedicated hours (the current 8 to 10 a week is a red flag [FACT]); a plan for the day job | MD decision |
| Is the company clean? | Incorporation, IP assignment, employer clearance, trademark filing | Before any term sheet |
