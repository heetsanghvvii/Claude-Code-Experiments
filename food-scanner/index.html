Food Label Checker

Look up a packaged food label and compare it with your own numbers. Data: Open Food Facts, your own targets and your own prices.

Type or paste the barcode digits printed on the pack, or scan the barcode with your camera. The camera image stays on this device and is not uploaded. If the scanner cannot read it, type the digits.

Your targets (optional)

Set your own numbers per 100 g. Nothing is preset. Leave a field empty to skip it. They are saved in this browser only.

The examples are only placeholders. These are your own targets and the app makes no health statement about them.
