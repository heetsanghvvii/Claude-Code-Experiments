06Questions

Fair questions.

Anything else, write to hello@knock.careers.

Is this spam?

No. We contact a hand-picked list of researched people, 60 in a Sprint, and every message is written for that one person. Spam is the same note sent to thousands. This is the opposite.

Will my LinkedIn get banned?

We stay well under LinkedIn's limits, and you send from your own account, at a human pace. No bots, no automation on your profile.

Do you write as me?

Yes, in your voice. And you approve every message before it goes. If something does not sound like you, we change it.

Who is this for?

To start, MBA graduates and final-year students, and professionals moving into product roles. If that is you, we would love to help.

What if nobody replies?

Founding members get a free extra week of outreach if fewer than 5 people reply.
