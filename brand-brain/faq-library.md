# FAQ Library (DM and comment reply templates)

Status: ASSUMED DEFAULTS, replace with your real brand. Engagement agent only uses these. Anything not covered gets escalated.
Pricing wording is a placeholder until real plans are published. Do not quote numbers from this file.

## Format
Each entry: trigger, reply, escalate-if.

### Pricing
- Trigger: "how much", "price", "cost", "pricing", "is it free"
- Reply: "Plans start at a few hundred dollars a month, depending on how many accounts and channels you run. Send me a DM with your channels and I will point you to the right plan."
- Escalate if: discount request, custom quote, enterprise or agency pricing, any specific number beyond "starts at a few hundred per month"

### Access and setup
- Trigger: "how do I start", "sign up", "get access", "waitlist", "how long to set up"
- Reply: "Sign up on the site and connect your accounts through each platform's official login. Most small accounts are set up within a day. Want me to send the setup checklist?"
- Escalate if: account is blocked, login fails, or the user asks for a timeline commitment

### How it works
- Trigger: "how does it work", "what does the agent do", "is it automatic"
- Reply: "A small team of agents drafts, checks and schedules your posts. You approve the batch each week, and they handle the rest. Nothing goes live without your approval unless you switch that on."
- Escalate if: the user asks for technical architecture or data flow details

### Which platforms
- Trigger: "tiktok", "x", "twitter", "pinterest", "does it do", "which platforms"
- Reply: "Right now we run Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn. Tell me which one you need and I will say whether it is on the list or coming."
- Escalate if: the user asks for a date on a new platform, or the platform is a competitor's stated roadmap item

### Privacy and account safety
- Trigger: "password", "is my data safe", "do you post as me", "permissions"
- Reply: "We never ask for your password. You connect through each platform's official login and can disconnect at any time. Your data is used to run your posts, not to train public models."
- Escalate if: a legal or security question, a breach report, or a request for the full data policy (send the policy link, do not paraphrase it)

### Collab / sponsorship
- Trigger: "collab", "partner", "sponsor", "shoutout", "affiliate"
- Reply: "Thanks for thinking of us. Send the details to partners@agentdesk.example and the team will reply within a few business days."
- Escalate: always

### Praise
- Reply options:
  1. "Thank you, that means a lot. Tell us what you are working on and we will help you map it out."
  2. "Glad it landed. What is the one post you want to fix first?"
  3. "Appreciate you saying so. Save it for the next time your Monday gets busy."

### Refund and cancellation
- Trigger: "refund", "cancel", "charged", "want my money back"
- Reply: "I am sorry to hear that. I am passing this to our team now so they can look at your account directly."
- Escalate: always (human only)

### Generic question not in library
- Action: escalate with a draft reply and a one-line summary of the question. Do not guess.

## Always escalate (never auto-answer)
- Refund, complaint, anger, legal threat, press, health/safety claim
- Any review under 4 stars
- Anything mentioning a minor, self-harm, or a competitor
- Anything requiring a price, promise, discount, timeline or policy not written here
- Any claim about results (followers, revenue, reach) the reader asks us to confirm
- Requests to post, DM or comment on someone else's behalf outside the approved calendar

## Tone for replies
Follow voice-guide.md. Reply length: 3 sentences max. Sign-off: none, reply as the account. Use the reader's first name only if they gave it. One emoji max, and none in refund or complaint replies.
