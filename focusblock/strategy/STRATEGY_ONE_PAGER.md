# nope strategy on one page

Author: CSO. Date: 9 Nov 2026. Labels: FACT (repo), ASSUMPTION, UNVERIFIED. Locked by the MD: Rs 1,999, brand nope (charcoal and saffron), broad audience on Instagram and Meta, Android first, no subscription.

## Who we serve first

Broad targeting stays (locked). The creative speaks to two moments, and the data tells us who answers.

- **Hero angle 1: "Leave it in another room."** The tap only works when you go to the puck. This is the product explained in 10 seconds and is the only thing a free setting cannot copy. Plan angle A1 [FACT].
- **Hero angle 2: study and exam focus** ("break time stays break time"). The simulated aspirant and student personas could justify Rs 1,999 as a study cost [SYNTHETIC, so a hypothesis only]. It also opens the institution channel in COMPETITION.md.
- **Angle to avoid: parents controlling teens, and anything that sounds like treating addiction.** The person holding the puck is the person being controlled, minors raise consent and data issues, and health or addiction claims are banned in our own rules [FACT, plan 5.4 and 3.3]. Also avoid any Brick comparison or price mention [FACT, rule].

Secondary, not led with: "pay once, no subscription" as a supporting line, gifting as a later seasonal test.

## Why we win (honestly)

Today we do not win structurally. We have a price (Rs 1,999 vs Rs 5,700 [FACT]), an India-first way of selling (UPI, WhatsApp, Hinglish), a calm brand, and a lock-out safety net built into the app [FACT]. These are a head start of months. We win if we use that head start to (1) prove the tap works on real phones, (2) prove strangers pay, (3) prove users still use it in week 4, and (4) lock in a context (students and institutions) that price cuts cannot take away.

## What we will not do (for the next 6 months)

- No iPhone build, Bluetooth, subscription, or custom hardware [locked or agreed in reviews]. Only the Apple paperwork clock may start (see REVIEW_SYNTHESIS D2).
- No health, addiction, or "unbreakable" claims. No fake testimonials or invented statistics. No named comparison with Brick.
- No third party analytics in the app (protects the "nothing leaves your phone" promise).
- No paid acquisition beyond the next step until the previous step passes.
- No Play Store launch date promised before the policy is read and a fallback is tested.
- No molded tooling before a confident forecast; the BOM math says payback is far off [FACT/my reading].
- No second product line, accessories (nest), or app store extras until 500 units.

## Next three bets (success and kill criteria)

All thresholds are ASSUMPTIONS from our own docs unless noted; change them when real numbers arrive.

### Bet 1. It works: the tap and the lock on real phones (to 20 Nov)

- Success: on at least 5 Android phones from different brands, including Android 13 and later, pairing, lock, and unlock work 9 times out of 10 through the printed shell; the sideload setup can be finished by a non-technical person in under 5 minutes.
- Kill or pivot: if the accessibility service cannot be enabled by a normal sideload user, or reads fail through the shell on most phones, stop all spend and fix before any ad. If no fix in 3 weeks, evaluate the fallback design (usage access plus overlay) or stop.

### Bet 2. Strangers pay: the demand ladder (from 24 Nov, per DEMAND_TEST)

- Success: a stranger from an ad pays (Rs 199 reservation or the full price). Step 3 (Rs 10,000 plus 10 real pucks) yields a cost per paid order below the corrected contribution, about Rs 1,135 on BOM inputs [my arithmetic, ASSUMPTION], with a target of under Rs 800 to leave room for founder time and support.
- Kill: the plan's own stop rule, fewer than 20 paid orders from a fair test, or cost per purchase above break even with no improving trend, or refunds above 20 percent [FACT, plan Q24]. Stop and rethink the offer, not the budget.

### Bet 3. It sticks: week 4 retention and the first moat signal (Dec to Jan)

- Success: week 4 active (locked on 4 or more of days 22 to 28) at or above 45 percent of delivered units, with 30 percent as the floor [FACT, METRICS]; refunds at or below 10 percent; plus one wedge signal (an institution or student group wants a pilot).
- Kill: week 4 active under 30 percent after 30 or more delivered pucks, or refunds above 20 percent. Then product is a novelty; do not scale ads.
