# Channel Playbooks

Status: ASSUMED DEFAULTS, replace with your real brand and data. Starting points only.
Timezone: America/New_York (all "best times" are Eastern)

## Instagram
- Active: yes. Handle: @agentdesk
- Formats: Reel (15-30s default), Carousel (6-10 slides), Single image, Story
- Caption length: Reel 80-150 chars + CTA; Carousel 150-300 chars
- Hook: first line of caption AND first 2 seconds of video must both carry it
- Hashtags: 3-5 niche tags (for example #solofounder #smallbusinessmarketing #contentcreator), no banned or spammy tags
- CTA style: comment keyword (BATCH, CALENDAR, AGENT) or save
- Cadence: 4 reels/week, 2 carousels/week
- Best times (ET): Tue 12:00, Thu 19:00, Sat 10:00
- Rules: no links in captions (use "link in bio"); one emoji max; no Stories auto-posting without a human check

## YouTube
- Active: yes. Channel: AgentDesk
- Formats: Shorts (under 45s), Long form (8-12 min)
- Title: under 60 chars, keyword first
- Description: first 150 chars carry keyword + hook, then timestamps, links
- Thumbnail: see visual-specs.md
- Cadence: 3 Shorts/week, 2 long form/month
- Best times (ET): Sat 09:00, Wed 17:00

## LinkedIn
- Active: yes. Page: AgentDesk
- Formats: Text post (900-1300 chars), Carousel/PDF, Short video
- Hook: first 2 lines must work before "see more"
- Hashtags: 0-3, at the end of the post only
- CTA style: question or opinion ask, one per post
- Cadence: 3 posts/week
- Best times (ET): Tue-Thu 08:00
- Rules: no external link in body (put in first comment); no emoji in the first two lines; reply to every comment within 24 hours

## Content pillars
Every post maps to exactly one pillar.
1. Build in public (target 30% of posts): what we built, broke, changed and measured at AgentDesk.
2. How-to automation (target 40% of posts): one repeatable step a solo creator or small brand can run this week, with the agent setup shown.
3. Results and lessons (target 30% of posts): a dated result from our own or a client's account (with permission), what it taught us, and what we changed.

## Weekly calendar minimum (used by thin-calendar interrupt)
- Instagram: 6 posts (4 Reels, 2 Carousels)
- YouTube: 3 Shorts
- LinkedIn: 3 posts

## Escalate the calendar if
- Any channel falls below its minimum two weeks running.
- A pillar is under 20% of posts for a full month.
