# Design: Knock

World: Indian inland letter card. Committed colour strategy.
- Colours: inland #A9C7E6, inland-light #C4D9EF, paper #EEF3F8, white #FAFCFE, ink #0E1F3A, ink-2 #2B4266, ink-3 #4A5F80, postal vermilion #B3301B (stamps, marks, hover, focus).
- Type: Archivo (display at wdth 70 to 72, weight 800; UI and body at wdth 100), Source Serif 4 (letters, the "To" line, captions in italic).
- Components: perforated stamp edges (radial-gradient ring), postmarks in SVG, a dashed fold line, pill buttons in navy that turn vermilion on hover, inputs with a bottom rule on the letter and boxed inputs in the form.
- Rhythm: 72 to 140px section padding, ink bands for How it works and the footer, inland bands for the example and the intake.
