How it works

Four steps, run by a person who does this every day. You stay in control of every word.

  1. You pick your targets

    Tell us the companies and roles you want. Ten companies is a good start. We help you sharpen the list if it is too broad.

  2. We find the right people at each

    Hiring managers, people on the team, alumni from your school, and senior people who can make an introduction. We research each one properly.

  3. We write a personal first message

    One message for one person, built on something you genuinely share: a school, a past company, a similar career move.

  4. We guide every reply

    When someone writes back, we draft your next message and keep the conversation going until it becomes a referral or an interview.

You approve every message. Nothing goes out without your yes, and it always goes from your own LinkedIn.

Why it works

60 hand-picked people. Not 6,000 messages.

  • Every message is written for one person

    No templates with a name swapped in. People can tell, and they reply to what feels real.

  • Conversation first, the ask comes later

    We open with a genuine question. Referrals follow once there is a reason to help you.

  • Built on how sales teams create meetings

    Good outbound teams book meetings with research and patience, not volume. We apply the same craft to your search.

What a first message looks like

Short, specific, and easy to answer. It reads like you, because you approve it.

  • A real shared path. Rahul made the move you want to make.
  • Your story in one line. Enough context, no CV.
  • A question, not an ask. Easy to reply to on a phone.
