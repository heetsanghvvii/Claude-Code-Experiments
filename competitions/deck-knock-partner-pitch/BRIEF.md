# Knock partner pitch deck brief

Every contestant builds from this brief alone. It is complete: never ask questions, never wait for approval.

## What it is
A 10 to 14 slide pitch deck that Knock's founder presents (or emails) to **placement committees and career/consulting/product clubs at Indian B-schools** (IIMs, ISB, XLRI, FMS, SPJIMR). Goal: get the club to partner with Knock, so Knock can offer its service to their students and alumni at a founding-member price, with the club promoting it.

## The product
**Knock** is a career outbound service. Instead of applying through job portals, Knock finds the right people at a candidate's target companies (hiring managers, team members, alumni, senior people who can introduce), researches each one, finds a genuine shared reason to talk, writes a short personal first message, and guides every reply until it becomes a referral or an interview. The candidate approves every message; it is sent from their own LinkedIn. It is a premium, human, tech-enabled service, not an "AI tool". Tagline: "Knock on the right door."

## Audience
Student committee heads (24 to 28, MBA, sharp, time-poor, protective of their batch's inbox and reputation) and a faculty or placement advisor. They have seen many ed-tech and "AI job tool" pitches and dismiss hype instantly.

## Storyline to cover (adapt order and wording; one idea per slide; action titles)
1. Title: Knock, Knock on the right door. Partner proposal for <Club name> (leave as a placeholder).
2. The problem: off-campus and lateral searches run through job portals; hundreds of applications, few replies. People hire people.
3. What already works: referrals and warm conversations convert far better than cold applications (state qualitatively; no invented numbers).
4. What Knock does, in 4 steps: pick target companies and roles; we find the right people; we write a personal first message for each based on something genuinely shared; we guide every reply to a referral or interview. You approve every message.
5. An example first message: "Hey Rahul, saw you moved from Flipkart into product at Zepto. I'm making a similar jump from brand management. Curious, what was the biggest adjustment for you?" with 2 or 3 notes on why it works.
6. Why it is not spam: 60 hand-picked people per week, every message personal, sent from the student's own account, well under LinkedIn's limits, conversations first and the ask later.
7. Packages (founding member pricing, INR): Sprint 7 days, 60 people, Rs 2,499; Standard 14 days, 120 people, Rs 3,999; Full search 21 days, 180 people, Rs 5,499; Done-for-you from Rs 14,999. Club members get the founding price.
8. The partnership offer: what the club gets (founding price for members, a free pilot for 5 students chosen by the club, a results summary the club can share, a co-hosted session on outbound job search) and what Knock asks for (one announcement to the batch, a feedback call after the pilot).
9. How we measure success: interviews per 100 people contacted, reported per student and in aggregate (no promises of numbers yet).
10. Pilot timeline: week 0 kickoff, weeks 1 to 2 outreach, week 3 results review.
11. Close: next step "Pick 5 students for the free pilot" and contact hello@knock.careers.

Add or merge slides if it strengthens the story, within 10 to 14 slides.

## Brand and voice
Calm, premium, human; private career concierge, not software. Short sentences, plain English, no hype, no exclamation marks, no em dashes. No robot imagery, no purple AI gradients. Visual direction is yours within that feel.

## Must not
Invent testimonials, customer names, logos, partner schools, statistics, conversion rates or results. Knock has no customers yet. Placeholders like <Club name> are fine.

## Technical rules
- Deliverable: `deck.pptx` in your entry folder: 16:9, native editable PowerPoint text and shapes (preferred). If your skill is HTML-native, deliver `deck.html` (each slide a 1920x1080 section that prints one slide per page) instead. Also write `NOTES.md` (direction in 3 lines, skill steps followed, problems).
- Python: `python-pptx` is installed. Node is available (npm works). LibreOffice is installed for rendering (`soffice --headless --convert-to pdf`).
- No external images (blocked); use native shapes, vector art, or clearly labelled image placeholders. Use fonts commonly available or embed-safe choices; Google Fonts load for HTML decks.
- Speaker notes are welcome but optional.
