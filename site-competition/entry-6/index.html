Questions

The honest answers.

Still unsure? Write to hello@knock.careers and a person will reply.

Is this spam?

No. A Sprint reaches 60 researched people, not thousands, and every message is written for one person. Spam is volume without a reason. We are the opposite.

Will my LinkedIn get banned?

We stay well under LinkedIn's limits, and you send from your own account at a human pace. No bots, no automation on your profile.

Do you write as me?

Yes, in your voice, and you approve every message before it goes. If something does not sound like you, we change it.

Who is this for?

To start, MBA graduates and final-year students, and professionals moving into product roles. If that is you, we would like to help.

What if nobody replies?

Founding members get a free extra week if fewer than 5 people reply. We would rather keep working than leave you waiting.
