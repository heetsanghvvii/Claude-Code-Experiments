**Positioning read [E]:** Nobody in India clearly owns "done for you, personalised, named-person outreach for MBAs/PMs". Closest functional substitutes are (a) DIY outreach with Premium plus Topmate sessions, (b) global reverse-recruiting services at Rs 2.5 lakh+/mo, (c) referral marketplaces that are cheap but low quality. Knock's gap: price Rs 2.5-15K against Rs 1.3 lakh+/mo for global done-for-you, with human research and reply management. Likely real competition: the job seeker's own time, and cheap freelance "LinkedIn outreach" services on Fiverr/Upwork/Instagram (not researched).

## 5. Risks

**LinkedIn automation and account restrictions [S]**
- User Agreement 8.2 bans scraping or copying profiles and data via crawlers, plugins or any software, and bans bots for sending messages or adding contacts. LinkedIn's help pages: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/90586 and https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387 (prohibited software and extensions).
- Enforcement: restriction, suspension or termination; restricted users are asked to disable the automation tool, after which access is auto-restored at the stated time (LinkedIn help).
- Vendor blogs (unreliable, self-interested) claim detection tightening in 2025 and bans of Apollo and Seamless data tools; weekly invite cap around 100 for ordinary accounts, 200-250 for strong-SSI Sales Navigator accounts [S, Findymail/SalesRobot/others via search].
- Implication for Knock [E]: a "self-send" product (customer sends manually from own account, Knock supplies researched targets and drafts) is far safer than Knock logging into customers' accounts. Done-for-you via the customer's login or any automation puts the customer's primary professional asset at risk and exposes Knock to ToS and reputational claims. Research should rely on manual/human review and non-scraped sources; avoid scraping LinkedIn at scale. Get legal advice before launching.

**DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025 [S]**
- DPDP Rules notified 14 Nov 2025. Rules 1, 2, 17-21 effective immediately; Rule 4 (consent managers) after 1 year; Rules 3, 5-16, 22-23 (notices, security, breach, children, etc.) after 18 months, i.e. approx **mid-May 2027**. Sources: PIB https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf ; Khaitan ERGO https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf ; Storyboard18, Deccan Herald.
- Section 3(c)(ii): Act does not apply to personal data made publicly available by the Data Principal themselves or under a legal obligation (Tsaaro, others). Profile data a person chose to publish is likely carved out, but: (a) stored notes, inferences, reply content, and the job seeker customers' own data (CV, targets, chats) are in scope; (b) enriched data from third-party sources is not clearly "made public by the principal"; (c) interpretation is untested. This is not legal advice [E].
- Practical: collect job-seeker consent and a purpose-specific notice, minimise and delete third-party data after the campaign, keep security safeguards and a breach process, publish grievance contact, avoid storing sensitive data, and appoint someone accountable. Prepare for obligations before May 2027 even though unenforced now.

**Reputation with recruiters / hiring managers [E]**
- Cold DMs from template-looking, mass-sent messages burn target-company goodwill and can get the sender (your customer) reported. Fix: low volume per company, genuinely personalised, opt-out respect, no more than 1-2 touches per person, no fake claims about relationships.
- Also: ethical risk if Knock "researches" alumni or team members in invasive ways; keep to professional public facts.

**Seasonality [S where noted]**
- Naukri JobSpeak: Oct 2025 white-collar hiring -9% YoY (index 2,480) due to Diwali/Dussehra clustering; underlying +3% holiday-adjusted; November +23% (index 3,001); Oct-Nov combined +6% [S] https://www.naukri.com/blog/naukri-jobspeak-report-white-collar-job-market-sees-temporary-dip-in-october-2025-due-to-festive-clustering/
- Diwali 2026 falls in early-to-mid November [M, verify], so the dip may shift to Nov 2026.
- B-school calendar [E/M]: summer internship processes run roughly Oct-Jan for first-years at top schools; final placements Dec-Feb (ISB Jan-Feb; IIMs Dec-Jan, with Day 0 cohorts in Nov-Dec); lateral hiring picks up Jan-Mar after bonuses/appraisals and again Jul-Sep. The best go-to-market windows: Sept-Nov (pre-season prep for final-year MBAs) and Jan-Mar (laterals). Dead zones: Diwali week, late Dec, May-June exam/vacation for students.

## 6. Strategic takeaways [E]
1. White space is price/format: Rs 2.5-15K, human-researched, named-person outreach for MBAs/PMs. Global done-for-you costs 10-25x more; Indian referral marketplaces and Topmate sell access or advice, not execution.
2. Lead with self-send for compliance, speed, and low price; use done-for-you only where the customer sends from their own account with approval of each message.
3. Partner rather than fight Nextleap-type bootcamps, PM communities, and Topmate mentors; they own audience and trust.
4. Time launch for the Sept-Nov pre-placement window; plan a lull around Diwali.
5. Proof drives price: collect response/referral rate data from pilot users and publish it.
