# The 15 riskiest assumptions, ranked

Author: CSO. Date: 9 Nov 2026. Ranked by (how badly it hurts if false) times (how little we know). Types: D desirability, F feasibility, V viability, X distribution. Costs come from repo files where possible; otherwise "low" (under about Rs 2,000, my estimate) or "unknown". Labels: FACT, ASSUMPTION, UNVERIFIED.

## Notes

- Tests 1, 2, 7 and 13 are the gate. Do not spend ad money until 1, 2 and 13 pass.
- Tests 3, 5, 6 and 10 are run together by the demand ladder, so budget once.
- Ranking is a judgment; if test 1 or 2 fails, ranks 3 to 11 become moot.
- Any pass bar here is my proposal and an ASSUMPTION; the CEO can change it before the test starts, not after.
