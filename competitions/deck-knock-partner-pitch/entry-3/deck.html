What already works

People hire people. Warm conversations open doors that cold applications cannot.

The cold application

  • One of hundreds in an applicant tracker
  • Judged on keywords before a human sees it
  • No context, no one vouching for you

The warm conversation

  • A real person who knows your name
  • Context on the team, the role and the timing
  • Often leads to a referral or a direct interview

Knock exists to start more of the second kind, for every student who asks.
