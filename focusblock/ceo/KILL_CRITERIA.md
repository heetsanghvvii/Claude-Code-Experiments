# Kill criteria

Author: CEO. Date: Mon 9 Nov 2026. All thresholds are ASSUMPTION chosen by the CEO or taken from the CMO ladder and NOPE_PLAN Q24. Changing them needs a written reason in DECISION_LOG.md.
Actions: PAUSE (stop spend or shipping, fix, resume), PIVOT (change the offer, angle or product), STOP (end the project or the channel), RETURN (give money back).

## A. Product

| ID | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | G0: 1 or fewer of 3 phones lock and unlock with a bare sticker, or Android 13+ phone cannot enable blocking after the guide steps | PAUSE all marketing and money pages. Engineering only. If the fallback usage mode also fails on 2 brands within 3 weeks (by Mon 7 Dec), STOP and report to the MD |
| K2 | G1: 2 or more of 5 phones fail 10 of 10 at 2.4 mm after one reprint, or fail with the felt pad fallback | PAUSE hardware sales. Redesign window 2 weeks. If still failing: STOP the hardware product |
| K3 | A blocking-service failure rate above 10 percent of pilot users (service killed, reported by check-ins) | PAUSE shipping beyond the pilot, fix per brand. If above 20 percent after fixes: PIVOT to usage mode only or STOP |
| K4 | Any customer locked out with no rescue for over 2 hours | PAUSE shipping. Fix the rescue route (COO and CCO open item) before the next unit |
| K5 | A phone brand fails more than half its tests | Add to a not-supported list on the page before the next ad dollar |

## B. Demand and ad spend

| ID | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| K6 | Hook test: all ads above Rs 50 per landing page view, or every hook rate under 15 percent after Rs 150 each | STOP this creative. No more money until a new hook is tested free as organic |
| K7 | Page views under 50 percent of link clicks | PAUSE ads, fix page speed or tracking. Read nothing else |
| K8 | Step 2: 0 or 1 paid from ad traffic after 200 landing page views | STOP paid ads. Organic and network only. Talk to 10 non-payers |
| K9 | Step 3: cost per paid order Rs 2,000 or more (4 or fewer orders on Rs 10,000) | STOP ads |
| K10 | Blended cost per paid order above Rs 850 for 7 days, or above Rs 600 when daily spend is raised | PAUSE spend increases. Above Rs 850 for 7 days: PAUSE ads |
| K11 | Cumulative ad spend reaches Rs 6,000 with fewer than 3 stranger-paid orders | PAUSE all paid acquisition |
| K12 | Fewer than 20 paid orders after a fair test (about Rs 18,000 of ads over 6 weeks) | STOP or PIVOT offer. RETURN unspent envelope cash to the MD. NOPE_PLAN Q24 says the same |
| K13 | Meta ad account restricted twice, or an ad flagged for claims twice | PAUSE ads. CLO review. Move to organic, creators and network |

## C. Money and refunds

| ID | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| K14 | Refund rate above 20 percent on 10 or more delivered orders | PAUSE shipping and ads. Find the reason. Above 25 percent: STOP scaling (the unit economics break) |
| K15 | Any day spend above the daily cap, or any rupee spent outside the envelope without MD yes | PAUSE all spend. CFO audit |
| K16 | Deposits and customer money mixed with business spend | PAUSE. CFO and CLO reconcile. Deposit money is never spent |
| K17 | Cash spent reaches Rs 10,000 with zero stranger-paid orders | RETURN: stop all spend, refund every reservation, report to the MD with what we learned. No further money without a new MD yes |

## D. Legal and platform

| ID | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| K18 | Employment contract claims IP or forbids the project and no written consent in 2 weeks | PAUSE everything public and money related. MD decides |
| K19 | Trademark search finds an identical or very close live mark in Class 9 or 42 | PAUSE paid ads until a lawyer opinion. Plan a modifier or a rename (a rename is a locked decision change and needs the MD) |
| K20 | Payment account frozen, or bank flags the UPI | PAUSE sales. Refund pending deposits |
| K21 | Play rejects the app twice AND the sideload path is blocked on target phones | PIVOT distribution (direct sideload with support) or STOP |

## E. Retention (the product-works test)

| ID | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| K22 | Week 4 active under 30 percent after 30 or more delivered pucks (non-replies count as not retained) | PAUSE ads. The product is a novelty. PIVOT (angle: study, gift, institution pilot) or STOP |
| K23 | Setup completed within 48 hours under 60 percent | PAUSE shipping. Fix setup before the next unit |
| K24 | G3: fewer than 3 of 5 watched strangers reach first lock | No shipping to paying customers |

## F. Founder and operations

| ID | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| K25 | Founder time per order above 90 minutes, or more than 15 orders a week with manual process (COO break line) | PAUSE ads. Helper or gateway checkout first |
| K26 | MD cannot give 6 hours a week for 2 weeks in a row | Stretch every date by the same ratio. Do not cut tests. Report in the briefing |

## What I do when a trigger fires

1. Pause first, analyse second. The pause is the CEO's decision and does not wait for the MD.
2. Write the cause, the number and the next test in DECISION_LOG.md the same day.
3. Tell the MD in the next briefing. Any money return or end of project is the MD's decision on my recommendation.
