Honest answers.

Which phones does it work with?

Android phones with NFC. Open Settings and search NFC to check. iPhone needs Apple's approval for this kind of app, so it comes later. We won't promise a date.

What exactly can the app see?

Only the name of the app that just opened. Not your screen, not your messages, not what you type. There is no account and nothing leaves your phone.

Will I get locked out of my phone?

No. nope only locks the apps you choose. Calls always work. You get two emergency unlocks a day, and your backup code gets you back in if the puck goes missing.

What if I lose the puck?

Enter your backup code in the app. It unlocks everything and lets you pair a new puck. That's why we ask you to save it in two places.

Can I cheat?

Yes. You can uninstall the app or switch off the permission. nope isn't a fortress. It adds one deliberate step, and often that is enough.

Does it need the internet?

No. Locking and unlocking happen between your phone and the puck.

Does it work through a phone case?

Thin cases are usually fine. Thick, metal or wallet cases can block the tap. If yours does, take the phone out for the tap.
