## Main card, BACK

**The one rule: leave it in another room.**

nope works because getting up to unlock is a small pause. A pause is enough to change your mind. If the puck sits next to your bed, it will not help. So:

1. Lock your phone when you decide to focus.
2. Walk to the other room. Put the puck down.
3. Come back. Do your thing.
4. When you are done, walk and tap. That walk is the point.

**Three places to leave it** (pick one tonight):

1. **The kitchen shelf or the spice rack.** You walk there anyway. Good for an evening lock after dinner.
2. **Your shoe rack or by the front door.** Good for work or study: lock as you arrive, unlock as you leave. Also stops the "just one reel" before stepping out.
3. **In a bag or locker at work or in your hostel, away from your desk.** Good for deep work hours. Pick something that takes you 30 seconds to reach, not 30 minutes. Too hard becomes a reason to quit.

Do not put it: in your pocket, on your bedside table, or inside your phone cover. Those are the same as not having it.

**Need your phone in an emergency?** Calls, messages and payments apps can be kept open. You also have 2 emergency unlocks a day (hold the button 10 seconds). If you ever lose the puck, use your backup code. [F: emergency unlock and backup code exist in the app]

**Not working for you?** You have 30 days. Message us and we refund, no fight. [F: 30 day refund]

## Ritual: "Leave it in another room", detailed

Do this for the first 7 nights. It takes 2 minutes.

1. Pick your lock time. Same time daily is best, for example 9 pm.
2. At that time, tap your phone on the puck. It says LOCKED.
3. Carry the puck to its spot (see 3 places on the back of the card). Leave it.
4. Do something with your hands: wash, stretch, read a page, charge the phone outside the bedroom.
5. When you want your phone back, walk to the puck. Notice the pause. Decide if you really need it. If yes, tap and use it.
6. Tell us tomorrow: "worked" or "did not". Even "did not" helps.

Honest note: for some people the ritual feels silly on day 1 and useful by day 5. For others it never clicks. Both are normal [A]. We will not pretend it works for everyone.
