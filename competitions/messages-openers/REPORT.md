# Message competition: report

7 approaches wrote openers and follow-ups for 10 test pairs. 4 blind Opus judges (recipients, hiring manager, Indian recruiter, rubric auditor).

| Rank | Entry | Approach | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | entry-4 | Knock engine: 3 writer agents + judge | 7.60 |
| 2 | entry-5 | Hybrid: research playbook + 3 angles + self-judge | 7.38 |
| 3 | entry-2 | Research playbook (33 rules) | 7.11 |
| 4 | entry-6 | Knock engine: single writer | 6.90 |
| 5 | entry-1 | Sales copywriting (Braun / McKenna) | 6.65 |
| 6 | entry-7 | Steve Dalton 5-point | 4.71 |
| 7 | entry-3 | Plain baseline | 3.50 |

Engine openers were the best (would reply to 8 of 10 as recipient). Hybrid had the best follow-ups (8.3 vs 6.5).

## Applied to the engine (engine/outreach/prompts.py)
- Openers: no "I'm Name" intro, concrete batch-year ties, practitioner questions that fail the 50-recipient test, "Dr. Lastname" for doctors, never claim unstated facts.
- Follow-ups: built on a second verified fact, insider insight line, either/or or menu questions, openly smaller ask, optional give-first offer, easy out.
- Multi-writer + judge stays the default (beat single writer by 0.7).
