# Knock launch video plan

**Format:** landscape 1920x1080, 30fps, 21 seconds. **Tone:** polished (restrained, long holds, soft staggered fades, no hard cuts).

## Angle
Job portals are a queue. People hire people. Knock finds the right people at your target companies, finds a genuine shared reason to talk, writes a personal first message you approve, and guides the replies. Tagline: "Knock on the right door." Aimed at anyone whose next job is at a company where referrals matter, so no MBA-specific copy. AI is never the headline and never mentioned.

## Hook
"Hundreds of applications. No callbacks." over the real application pile from the landing page, ending with the red "Still waiting / Return to sender" stamp thudding down.

## Highlights
1. Reveal: the real airmail hero sheet. "Stop applying. Start conversations." The stamp and postmark land, "To" field types Zepto, Razorpay, Swiggy, button press.
2. A shared reason to talk: the real target list ledger, three rows, the shared reason highlighted in the postal red wash (labelled sample, as on the page).
3. A first message you approve: the real example note typing in, three highlighted parts, then "Approved by you / Sent from your LinkedIn".

## Punchline
Dark navy outro: the four real "How it works" steps light up, then "Knock on the right door." with "Stop applying. Start conversations." and the real "Get my target list" button, "Packages from Rs 2,499".

## Visual identity
Taken from brand/tokens.css and deploy/public/index.html, reusing the page's own CSS and markup: inland powder blue #A9C7E6, paper #EEF3F8, navy ink #0E1F3A, postal red #B3301B, Archivo condensed display and Source Serif 4 (fetched from Google Fonts and served locally).

## Storyboard (sums to 21.0s)
| Scene | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | 0.0 to 3.6 | Headline, application pile staggers in, stamp slam at 1.75 |
| 2 Reveal | 3.6 to 8.3 | Sheet fades in after scene 1 is gone, headline lines, stamp, postmark, typing, click |
| 3 Shared reason | 8.3 to 12.4 | Hero content out, then headline and ledger rows in one by one |
| 4 First message | 12.4 to 17.5 | Sheet dissolves to inland blue, note types in, approval ticks |
| 5 Outro | 17.2 to 21.0 | Dip to navy, four steps, tagline, button |

Transitions are staggered (old content out, then new in, dipping through the background) to avoid double exposures.

## Sound
Original, synthesised with numpy: warm D major pad (Dmaj9, Bm9, Gmaj7, A), soft plucked eighth-note arpeggio, soft bass, reverb. SFX are in the same key: low stamp thuds, quiet typing ticks, a button click, bell chimes on D pentatonic for ledger rows, the approval and the four steps, and a resolving bell chord on the tagline. Effects sit under the music.
