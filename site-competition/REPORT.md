## Grafts for the final site
- Keep entry-9's inline "To:" field (all 3 judges named it the best conversion idea).
- Entry-1's annotated example message and 60-dot grid.
- Entry-2's honesty line ("We do not guarantee replies. We do guarantee the work") and card footers ("Approved by you / Sent from your LinkedIn").
- Calm it down: smaller stamp on mobile, better caption contrast, no autocomplete=off.
- Never hide content behind opacity-0 scroll reveals (7 of 12 entries failed full-page capture because of this).

## Notes
- Judges: design director, target user, accessibility auditor (all Opus). The target-user and accessibility judges preferred entry-2's warm green palette; the design director called it generic.
- Process lesson for skill-competition: tell builders to show content by default and use motion only as an enhancement.
