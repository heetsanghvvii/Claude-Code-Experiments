## Decision tree (from CMO, with my overlay)

- Day 3: G0 failed: do not publish Day 4. Hook test moves to the day after G0 passes.
- Day 6 Step 0: 3 or more paid or committed signals from outside close family: GO. 1 or 2: FIX the top objection. 0 and interviews show the demo is unclear: STOP spending. Note: before G2 there are no payments, so Step 0 counts waitlist joins that include phone model and "I would pay Rs 1,999" replies; paid reservations start from Wed 18 Nov. The Step 0 gate is therefore re-read on Fri 20 Nov with reservations.
- Day 11 Step 1: best ad cost per landing page view Rs 25 or less GO; Rs 25 to 50 FIX; above Rs 50 on all ads or hook rate under 15 percent on all: STOP this creative, no more money.
- Step 2 (about Day 21 to 29): 6 or more paid from ad traffic after 200 landing page views GO; 2 to 5 FIX; 0 or 1 STOP.
- Step 3: cost per paid order Rs 850 or less AND pilot guardrails pass GO; Rs 850 to 2,000 FIX; Rs 2,000 or more STOP. Note CEO ceiling: scaling needs Rs 600 or less.
