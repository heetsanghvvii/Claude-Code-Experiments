# Theme: Brass Door (custom)

Created because none of the 10 preset themes fit a premium, human career concierge. Closest presets were Forest Canopy (grounded greens) and Golden Hour (warmth); Brass Door blends the calm of the first with the warmth of the second and pushes contrast up to WCAG AA.

## Color Palette

- **Ink Green**: `#14302A` - Primary dark: hero, featured package, footer
- **Paper**: `#F6F1E7` - Main background, warm ivory
- **Linen**: `#ECE4D3` - Secondary surfaces, alternating sections
- **Brass**: `#D4A458` - Accent on dark surfaces (door light, CTA on ink)
- **Deep Brass**: `#87591A` - Accent text on light surfaces (5.1:1 on Paper)
- **Charcoal**: `#1D2320` - Body text on light (15:1 on Paper)
- **Moss Grey**: `#4C5A53` - Secondary text on light (6.7:1 on Paper)

## Typography

- **Headers**: Fraunces (soft, optical serif; 500 to 600 weight)
- **Body Text**: Figtree (humane sans, 400 to 600)

## Best Used For

Premium services, concierge and advisory brands, editorial landing pages.
