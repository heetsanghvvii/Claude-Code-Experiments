Food Label Checker

Private prototype. Data: Open Food Facts and your own label photos.

Type or paste the barcode digits printed on the pack. This does not use the camera.

Examples:

1. Photo of the ingredient label

Reads printed text from a photo using OCR. It does not read barcodes. Text is read on this device with Tesseract.js (script loaded from a CDN when you press the button). The photo is not uploaded anywhere.
