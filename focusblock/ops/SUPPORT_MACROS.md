**M2. Accessibility greyed out ("restricted setting")**
That grey switch is Android's protection for apps installed outside the Play Store. Fix: 1) Long press the nope app icon, tap App info. 2) Tap the three dots at the top right, tap "Allow restricted settings" and confirm with your PIN. 3) Go to Settings > Accessibility > nope > turn on. Menu names differ a bit by brand. Send a screenshot if you do not see the three dots.

Cases with a metal ring, metal plate or a magnet ring can block the NFC signal. Easiest test: take the case off and tap again. If it works, keep the case off while tapping, or use a thin plain case. Do not put the puck on a magnet. Let me know the phone and case and I will note it down.

**M6. App stopped blocking after a few hours (battery optimisation)**
This is usually the phone putting nope to sleep to save battery. Please set nope to "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions" under Settings > Apps > nope > Battery. Also lock nope in the Recents screen (long press the app card, tap the lock). Quick brand guide, names vary [U]: Samsung: Battery > Background usage limits > remove nope from "Sleeping apps". Xiaomi/Redmi/Poco: Autostart ON and Battery saver > No restrictions. Oppo/Realme/OnePlus: Allow auto launch and Allow background activity. Vivo/iQOO: Background power consumption > Allow. Motorola/Pixel: Battery > Unrestricted. Tell me your brand and I will send the exact steps.
