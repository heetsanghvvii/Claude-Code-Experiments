## Where Phase 1 sales actually happen (assumption)
50 units to college students and exam aspirants means selling mostly person to person: hostel floors, library desks, coaching WhatsApp groups, Instagram DMs. So the biggest levers are: a demo the founder can do in 20 seconds, a poster, a WhatsApp message, and a trust page. Polished brand video is not needed yet.

## Things only the founder can unblock (blockers for copy)
1. What happens if the puck is lost or damaged (recovery method). Personas feared this most.
2. What data the app collects or sends. Say it plainly on the page.
3. Real company name, city, contact and GST/invoice status.
4. The exact accessibility permission use and what the app does not read.
5. Whether uninstalling the app unlocks everything (be honest either way).
6. Units: do you hand over in person at campuses, or ship? Landing page says shipping in about 2 to 3 weeks for the first batch.
