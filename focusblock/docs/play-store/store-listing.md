## Full description (4,000 characters max)

FocusBlock helps you stay off the apps that eat your time.

Pick the apps that distract you. Then stick a Brick, a small NFC sticker, somewhere you can reach it, like your desk or your bedside table. Tap it with your phone to start a focus block. Tap it again to stop.

While a block is on, opening a blocked app sends you straight back to your Home screen. Your phone stays usable for everything else.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose the apps to block.
2. Pair one or more NFC stickers (NTAG213 or NTAG215 work well).
3. Turn on the FocusBlock Accessibility service once, so it can notice when a blocked app opens.
4. Tap your sticker to lock. Tap it again to unlock.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Works entirely on your phone.
- No account or sign-up.
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking.
- Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

ABOUT THE ACCESSIBILITY PERMISSION
FocusBlock uses the Accessibility service for one thing. It sees which app has just come to the front, checks it against your list, and sends you Home if it is blocked. It does not read what is on your screen, what you type, or your passwords. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings.

NFC stickers are sold separately. Any NTAG213 or NTAG215 sticker will work.
